How to climb

Every day there's a new route with 7 geography questions. Each one has many right answers — you get one try per question.

The less common your right answer, the harder the hold you grabbed and the higher you climb:

  • Jug · 5.7 · the answer most people give · +100 m
  • Edge · 5.10 · good thinking · +200 m
  • Crimp · 5.12 · not many people think of it · +300 m
  • Mono · 5.14 · a deep cut! · +400 m
  • Slip · not a right answer · +0 m

Small typos are fine. Climb all 7 perfectly with Monos to top out at 2,800 m.
