How to climb

Every day there's a new route at a real crag somewhere in the world, with 5 geography questions. Each has lots of right answers — you get one try and 30 seconds.

The rarer your right answer, the harder the hold — and the higher you climb.

  • JugThe answer most people give
    +100 m
  • SloperGood thinking
    +200 m
  • CrimpNot many people find it
    +300 m
  • Foot ChipThe rarest answer of all
    +400 m
  • SlipWrong answer or out of time
    +0 m

Small typos are fine. Five Foot Chips tops out at 2,000 m.
